C19 is currently not accepting new submissions. 

We are completing the publications already in progress, and will be taking a hiatus during the Fall 2026 semester. We will post on this page with information about when the submission portal will be open again.

Feel free to prepare your data in the meantime, following best practices for data publications outlined in the "[Criteria](/criteria)" tab of the website. For submission, you should have:

- A ["tidy" dataset](/criteria/dataset-preparation) in a stable format.
- Complete [documentation](/criteria/data-documentation) about your dataset to support reuse.
- A polished [data essay](/criteria/data-essay).
- Information gathered to ensure [rich metadata](/criteria/metadata).
